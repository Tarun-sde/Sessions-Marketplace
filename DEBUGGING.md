# Debugging Log

This log documents real technical issues, failed assumptions, and bugs encountered during development and testing.

Format per issue:
- **Symptom**: What failed or behaved unexpectedly.
- **Diagnosis**: How the problem was investigated and observed.
- **Root Cause**: The underlying technical reason.
- **Fix**: The code/configuration change applied.
- **Verification**: How the fix was proven to work.
